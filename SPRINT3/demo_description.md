# Sprint 3 Demo – MealMajor

**Date:** March 27, 2026

## What We're Demonstrating

New features added to MealMajor including a meal planner calendar, AI-powered recipe chat, a recipe game, and overall UI/UX improvements.

## Demo Flow

### 1. Meal Planner Calendar
- Assign recipes to specific days and meal sections (breakfast, lunch, dinner).
- Edit or remove meals directly from the calendar view.

### 2. AI Chatbot
- Chat with an AI assistant to get recipe suggestions based on your saved recipes.
- Powered by the Gemini API on the backend.

### 3. Recipe Game ("Unique")
- Launch the tournament-style recipe picker from the navbar.
- Swipe through recipe cards in a bracket format to pick a winner.
- Confetti animation celebrates the final selection.

### 4. Recipe Popup & Ingredients
- View full recipe details in a popup overlay.
- Select ingredients from a predefined list when creating recipes.

### 5. UI/UX Improvements
- Loading screen added for smoother transitions.
- Dark mode fixes for readability across all pages.
- Improved recipe form validation and error handling.
