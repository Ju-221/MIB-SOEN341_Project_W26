## Problem

University students often struggle to eat healthy and stay organized with their meals. 
They have limited time, tight budgets, and no structured way to plan what they will eat, 
track the groceries they need, and avoid wasting food. Existing tools are either too 
complicated, too generic, or not tailored to student life.

## Proposed Solution

MealMajor is a web application that helps students plan their weekly meals, manage 
their recipes, and keep track of groceries in a simple and intuitive way. 
The app allows users to:

- Create and manage an account with personalized diet preferences and allergies.
- Store, search, and filter simple recipes.
- Plan their meals for the week in a clear view.

In Sprint 1, we focus only on the core User Account Management features: 
registration, login, and basic profile management (diet preferences, allergies).
